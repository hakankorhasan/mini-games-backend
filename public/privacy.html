Privacy Policy

Last Updated: Apr 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to BrainLand: Brain Puzzle Games. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle any information you provide while using our application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Data Collection and Usage

BrainLand is designed to be largely offline-first. We do not require you to create an account. However, to provide features such as leaderboards, cloud saves, and anonymous analytics, your gameplay progress, device identifiers, and certain non-personally identifiable information may be stored locally or transmitted to secure third-party backend servers (like Firebase and Apple iCloud).

3. Advertisements

Our application contains advertisements to help us keep the core features free. We work with third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob) which may collect and use data about your interactions with the app, device identifiers (like the App Tracking Transparency IDFA on iOS), IP address, and location data to serve both targeted and non-targeted advertisements. You can modify your advertising preferences directly from your device's privacy settings.

4. In-App Purchases

Our app offers In-App Purchases (such as premium access or ad-removal). We do not collect or store the financial information associated with your transactions. All payments are securely processed by the Apple App Store, and we only receive anonymous confirmation tokens to unlock in-game features.

5. Third-Party Services

We use third-party tools such as Firebase for backend services (Leaderboards, real-time database, analytics) and various Ad Providers for monetization. These third parties have their own Privacy Policies dictating how they handle data.

6. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your data by heavily relying on industry-standard and platform-provided (Apple/Google) secure frameworks to ensure any data transmitted remains safe.
